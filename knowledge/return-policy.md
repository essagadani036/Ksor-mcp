Official product return terms for all purchases made through the E-commerce Mall. Customers, fulfillment staff, and shopping assistants must follow these conditions.

## Eligibility and Conditions

### 1. 7-Day Return Window

- Returns are strictly permitted within **7 calendar days of delivery**.
- The return window starts from the exact timestamp confirmed by the courier delivery tracking system.
- Return requests initiated after the 7th calendar day cannot be accepted.

### 2. Product Condition Requirements

To qualify for an authorized return:
- **Unused and Unworn**: The item must never have been worn outdoors, laundered, altered, or used beyond an initial indoor fitting.
- **Original Condition**: The item must be returned in its original brand packaging, with all original tags, barcode stickers, care labels, spare buttons, and protective dust bags intact and unsevered.
- Items exhibiting signs of perfume scent, smoke, deodorant marks, stains, scuffs, or fabric snags will fail quality inspection.

### 3. Qualified Reasons for Return

A return can be initiated under the following circumstances:
- **Wrong Product**: The delivered item does not match the item, size, color, or variant ordered.
- **Damaged in Transit**: The parcel or item arrived with visible transit damage.
- **Defective or Faulty**: The product exhibits manufacturing flaws, missing hardware, broken zippers, or torn seams upon delivery.
- **Sizing or Fit Issue**: The customer ordered their standard size but requires a different fit.

### 4. Size Exchange Guidelines

- If an item does not fit, the customer may request a **size exchange** instead of a straight refund.
- Size exchanges are offered **if the required size is available in current inventory**.
- If the required replacement size is out of stock, the customer will be routed to the standard return and refund process.
- Only one complimentary size exchange is permitted per purchased item.

## Return Process

1. **Initiate Request**: The customer submits a return request through the customer portal or through the AI Shopping Agent within 7 days of delivery.
2. **Document Condition**: For damaged, defective, or incorrect items, photographic evidence of the item and courier packing slip must be uploaded.
3. **Pickup or Drop-off**: A reverse pickup is scheduled with our courier partner, or the parcel is dropped off at a designated logistics hub.
4. **Warehouse Inspection**: Upon delivery to our fulfillment facility, warehouse inspectors verify that all condition criteria are met before passing the case to the refund department.
