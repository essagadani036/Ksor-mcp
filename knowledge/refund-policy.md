Official refund policy and disbursement standards for the E-commerce Mall. All customer refunds, partial return deductions, and shipping fee compensations are governed by these rules.

## Core Refund Rules

### 1. Mandatory Inspection Prerequisite

- A refund is issued **only after the returned product has been physically received and checked** at our central warehouse.
- The quality assurance team inspects the item to confirm that it is unused, unworn, complete with tags, and matches the original return declaration.
- Inspection is completed within 2 to 3 business days of package arrival.

### 2. Refund Amount Limits

- The refund is strictly limited to the **amount actually paid** by the customer for the returned item.
- Value added by promotional discounts, store credits, vouchers, or markdown price reductions is excluded from the refund sum.
- If an item was received free of cost under a promotional bundle, its refundable value is 0 PKR.

### 3. Recalculation of Buy 4, Get 1 Free for Partial Returns

When an order originally qualified for the **Buy 4, Get 1 Free** promotion and the customer requests a partial return, the promotional benefit is retroactively re-evaluated:

- **Dropping Below Threshold**: If the number of retained products in the order falls below the 5-item qualification threshold (e.g., customer kept 4 items and returned 1), the customer no longer qualifies for the free 5th item.
- **Deduction Mechanism**: The monetary value of the free item originally credited at checkout is deducted from the refund payout of the returned paid items.
- **Returning the Free Item Alone**: If the customer returns only the free promotional item, the refund amount is **0 PKR**, and the 4 paid items remain unaffected.
- **Retaining 5+ Items**: If an order originally had 10 items (qualifying for 2 free items) and 2 paid items are returned leaving 8 retained items, the customer still qualifies for 1 free item. The value of the second free item is adjusted against the refund.

#### Partial Return Calculation Example

1. **Initial Order**:
   - Item 1: 5,000 PKR
   - Item 2: 4,000 PKR
   - Item 3: 3,000 PKR
   - Item 4: 2,000 PKR
   - Item 5 (Cheapest, Free): 1,000 PKR
   - Total Paid: 14,000 PKR (15,000 PKR original price minus 1,000 PKR discount).
2. **Customer Returns Item 1 (5,000 PKR)**:
   - Retained Items: Item 2 (4,000 PKR), Item 3 (3,000 PKR), Item 4 (2,000 PKR), Item 5 (1,000 PKR). Total retained = 4 items.
   - Qualification Check: 4 retained items do not qualify for the Buy 4 Get 1 Free promotion. Item 5 is now a full paid item at 1,000 PKR.
   - Revised Retained Cost: 4,000 + 3,000 + 2,000 + 1,000 = 10,000 PKR.
   - Refund Due: Total Paid (14,000 PKR) minus Revised Retained Cost (10,000 PKR) = **4,000 PKR** (not the full 5,000 PKR price of Item 1, because the 1,000 PKR promotional grant was rescinded).

### 4. Delivery Charges Policy

- **Seller Fault (Full Delivery Refund)**: When the seller sent the **wrong product**, or when the product arrived **damaged or defective**, 100% of the original shipping and delivery charges are refunded to the customer. Any return courier fees are also borne entirely by the store.
- **Customer Preference / Size Returns**: If the return is due to personal preference, mind change, or voluntary return where the goods delivered matched the order specifications, initial delivery charges are non-refundable.

### 5. Payout Methods and Timelines

- Approved refunds are credited back to the original payment source (Bank Transfer, Credit/Debit Card, or Mobile Wallet such as EasyPaisa/JazzCash) within 5 to 7 business days following inspection clearance.
- For Cash on Delivery (COD) orders, refunds are disbursed via direct bank transfer or store credit wallet upon receipt of verified customer account details.
